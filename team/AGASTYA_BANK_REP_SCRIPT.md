# Advocall: Live Demo Bank Rep Script
**Role:** Priya (HDFC Bank Customer Support Representative)  
**Actor:** Agastya  
**Demo Case:** Failed UPI transfer of ₹4,500 (Ref: UPI4829301) on behalf of customer Ravi Kumar.

---

## Live Call Dialogue & Cues

### Cue 1: The AI Greets and States the Case
**Advocall (AI):**  
> *"Hello, this is Advocall calling on behalf of Ravi Kumar regarding a failed UPI transfer of ₹4,500 on 22 September, reference UPI4829301. The amount was debited from his account but not credited to the beneficiary. We need a complaint number and reversal date."*

---

### Step 1: The Classic Brush-Off (Line 1)
**Priya (Agastya - Speak smoothly in typical call center tone):**  
> *"Sir/Madam, in UPI issues the amount usually gets auto-credited back to the original account within 7 to 10 working days. I request the customer to please wait until next week."*

---

### Cue 2: Advocall Pushes Back with RBI Law
**Advocall (AI):**  
> *"As per RBI's turnaround-time rules, a failed UPI debit must be reversed by T+1, with ₹100 per day compensation after that. Could you please register a complaint and give me the complaint number and the reversal date?"*

---

### Step 2: The Concession & Commitment (Line 2)
**Priya (Agastya - Polite, professional, compliant):**  
> *"Understood. Let me register a formal grievance for this transaction. Please note down the complaint docket number: **CMP88213**. The funds will be reversed to the customer's account by **29 September 2026**, along with the applicable statutory delay compensation as per banking guidelines."*

---

### Cue 3: Advocall Verifies and Closes
**Advocall (AI):**  
> *"Thank you. I have recorded complaint number CMP88213 with reversal promised by 29 September 2026 including statutory compensation. Have a good day."*
