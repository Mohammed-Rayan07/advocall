# Advocall: 3-Minute Pitch Script
**Speaker:** Agastya · **Target:** 3:00 (~400 spoken words) · audited at the checkpoint merge: every sentence matches what the prototype really does.
**Numbers change with the date:** the engine counts days late from *today*. On 26 Sep it is 3 days / ₹300 / ₹4,800; after midnight it becomes 4 days / ₹400 / ₹4,900. **Read the numbers off the screen.**
**Before pitching:** check NPCI's latest monthly UPI number and put it in line 3, or keep "billions".

---

### [0:00 – 0:30] 1. The hook
> "Good afternoon, judges.
>
> India makes **billions of UPI payments every month**. And most of us have had this message: ₹4,500 debited, but the shopkeeper never got it.
>
> So you call customer care. Twenty minutes of hold music, and then: *'Sir, please wait 7 to 10 working days.'*
>
> Almost nobody knows that under **RBI's 2019 circular on failed transactions**, that money must be reversed by **T+1**, and after that the bank owes you **₹100 for every day of delay**. People don't claim it, because nobody has the time to fight."

### [0:30 – 1:20] 2. Advocall, live
> "Meet **Advocall**: an AI that waits on hold for you.
>
> You tell it your problem in **Hindi, Kannada or English**. It calls the company for you, says upfront that it's an AI calling on your behalf, gets through the IVR, pushes back politely when they brush you off, and doesn't hang up without a **complaint number and a date**. Then it calls you back in your language.
>
> *(Dashboard on screen. LIVE: Rayan's phone intake → Advocall calls me, I play HDFC's 'Priya'. BACKUP: Run demo → UPI Hero dispute.)*
>
> Watch the right side: I try the classic brush-off, *'wait 7 to 10 days'*, and Advocall answers with the RBI rule. I give in: complaint **CMP88213**, reversal by **29 September**. It reads the number back, and the ticket appears on the dashboard. Then it calls the customer back in Hindi."

### [1:20 – 2:05] 3. The rights engine: why you can trust it
> "Here's the part we're proudest of. **The AI never does the maths and never picks the law.**
>
> Our **rights engine** is plain code. It takes the facts from the call and computes the deadline, how many days late, and the compensation: for this case, 3 days late, **₹300 owed, ₹4,800 at stake**. Every claim links to its official source.
>
> The voice agent receives just one approved legal sentence. If a rule isn't verified yet, like telecom, the engine gives it **no** legal claim, and the agent only asks for a complaint number and a date."

### [2:05 – 2:35] 4. Safety
> "We built in hard limits:
> - It **never asks for or accepts an OTP, PIN or password**.
> - It pushes back **at most twice**. It is polite, never threatening.
> - If the company refuses, Advocall prepares a **complaint letter for the RBI Ombudsman or the National Consumer Helpline**, with a key-facts table, ready for the customer to file.
>
> *(If time: click 'Deadline missed: escalate' to show the letter.)*"

### [2:35 – 3:00] 5. Impact and team
> "Every failed payment, stuck refund or wrong bill is a small fight people give up on. Advocall fights it for them, in their language.
>
> Built at this hackathon by:
> - **Rayan**, lead engineer: architecture, live event pipeline and the voice calls;
> - **Vaishnavi**: the live dashboard;
> - **Yaso**: Hindi and Kannada content, demo scenarios and QA;
> - and me, **Agastya**: the rights engine and the legal research.
>
> Stop waiting on hold. **Advocall waits for you.** Thank you!"
